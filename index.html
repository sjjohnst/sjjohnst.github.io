About Me

Hello! My name is Sam.
I am passionate about the natural world, and trying to solve problems that are impacting the environment and climate. I work a lot with earth observation, remote sensing and geospatial data, and collaborate within interdisciplinary teams to tackle these issues. My technical expertise spans Software Development, Machine Learning, Data Science and Scientific Research.

Currently, I am the Lead Software Developer at H2O Geomatics. In this role, I contribute to research and data production for the ESA CCI Lakes project, lead a development team for a biodiversity monitoring web application, and conduct research into deep learning approaches to lake systems modelling.

I also work as an independent contractor on the side (but unfortunately cannot say much more now). You can check out my Resume here.

In my freetime, I am an outdoor enthusiast, most especially for rock climbing and camping. I enjoy playing the guitar and writing little songs, as well as sewing, embroidery, and cooking.
